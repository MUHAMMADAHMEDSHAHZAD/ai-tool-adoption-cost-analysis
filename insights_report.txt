
AI TOOL ADOPTION & COST ANALYSIS - INSIGHTS REPORT
=====================================================

1. TOP PERFORMING DEPARTMENT
   Sales department delivers the highest ROI (215.66%) with relatively
   low total spend ($26,241). This suggests Sales team uses AI tools
   most efficiently for revenue-generating tasks.

2. UNDERPERFORMING DEPARTMENT
   HR shows the lowest ROI (159.64%) despite moderate spending.
   Recommendation: Review which AI tools HR uses and whether tasks
   are well-suited for automation, or provide additional training.

3. BEST AI TOOL BY ROI
   Claude leads with 202% average ROI, followed by Midjourney (196%)
   and GitHub Copilot (194%). ChatGPT has the lowest ROI (161%) despite
   being widely used - suggesting cost may not match output value in
   its current use cases.

4. COST EFFICIENCY
   Finance has the highest cost-per-employee ($42.53), while R&D has
   the lowest ($33.39). High per-employee cost does not always
   correlate with high ROI.

5. ADOPTION TREND
   AI adoption spend shows seasonal spikes (Sep-Oct 2025, July 2026)
   and dips (Nov 2025-Feb 2026), likely tied to fiscal year budget
   cycles. Recommendation: Investigate cause of dips and plan
   consistent adoption strategy across quarters.

6. KEY BUSINESS RECOMMENDATION
   Reallocate a portion of underperforming departments' AI budget
   (e.g., HR, Marketing) toward tools and practices used successfully
   by Sales and Finance. Standardize on Claude and GitHub Copilot
   where possible, given their consistently higher ROI.
